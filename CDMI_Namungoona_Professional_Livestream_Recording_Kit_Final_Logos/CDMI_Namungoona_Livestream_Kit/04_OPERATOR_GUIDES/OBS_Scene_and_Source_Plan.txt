- Recording: MKV; remux to MP4 after checking the MKV

FILTERS
MICROPHONE: Noise Suppression (light) → Expander OR Noise Gate (gentle) → Compressor (moderate) → Limiter (ceiling around -1 dB).
CAMERA: correct lighting and white balance first; mild Color Correction only if needed; Crop/Pad for framing; Chroma Key only for green screen.
TICKER: Scroll filter on the text source. Keep the speed slow enough to read.

IMPORTANT
Filter values depend on the actual microphone, room, mixer and PC. Listen to a recording before the public stream. Use OBS View > Stats to monitor encoding lag, rendering lag and network dropped frames.
