CDMI NAMUNGOONA — RECORDING & ARCHIVE WORKFLOW

RECOMMENDED FILE FORMAT
- Record to MKV in OBS for crash resilience.
- After recording, use OBS File > Remux Recordings to create MP4 if needed.
- Keep the MKV master until the MP4 has been checked.

FILE NAMING
YYYY-MM-DD_CDMI_[SERVICE]_[CAMERA/PROGRAM].mkv
Example: 2026-11-22_CDMI_Sunday_Service_Master.mkv

FOLDER STRUCTURE
CDMI_RECORDINGS/
  2026/
    2026-11/
      2026-11-22_Sunday_Service/
        MASTER_MKV/
        MP4_EXPORT/
        SHORT_CLIPS/
        THUMBNAILS/
        NOTES/

AFTER RECORDING
1. Confirm file size is non-zero and playback works.
2. Listen to the start, middle and end.
3. Check audio/video sync.
4. Make a second copy on another drive or secure cloud location.
5. Keep an archive register with date, service, speaker, scripture and publishing approval.
6. Do not upload recordings containing private counselling, prayer requests, children or sensitive information without appropriate approval and safeguards.

PUBLISHING
- Confirm church leadership approval.
- Confirm rights for music and any third-party video/graphics.
- Check title, date, speaker, scripture and thumbnail.
- Review the full recording or an approved edit before publishing.
